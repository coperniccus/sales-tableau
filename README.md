# Tableau Sales Dashboard

A Tableau dashboard tracking core sales metrics and performance, analyze product profitability, and analyze customer behavior.

[Tableau Public link](https://public.tableau.com/views/sales-tableau_17873971199980/SalesDashboard?:language=en-US&publish=yes&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)


## Dashboard Overview

### Sales & Profitability
* **KPI Summary**: Total Sales, Profit, and Quantity with sparklines comparing performance to the previous year.
* **Subcategory Breakdown**: Bar-in-bar chart comparing gross sales revenue against net profit per subcategory.
* **Multi-Year Trends**: Continuous line chart tracking multi-year sales and profit trajectories over time.

### Customer Behavior
* **KPI Overview**: Total Customers, Total Orders, and Average Sales per Customer (Current Year vs. Prior Year).
* **Order Distribution**: Customer segmentation based on order frequency to measure engagement and loyalty.
* **Top Customers by Profit**: Ranked list of the most profitable customers, detailing order counts, total spend, and latest order dates.
